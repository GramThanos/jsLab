jsLab

Virtual machines and networks in the browser, on the TinyEMU WebAssembly engine. Nothing runs on a server.

Demos

  • Multi-VM demo: several isolated VMs (on the page or in workers) on one virtual LAN with DHCP.
  • Single VM: boots a staged distro, no dependencies.
  • Legacy single-VM UI, kept as a reference.

The demos boot the guest images staged in _distro/ (with TinyEMU-distros' scripts/stage-web.sh) or, when there are none, the x86 distros TinyEMU-distros publishes. demo.html also takes the URL of any config.

Library and docs

lib/vm (VMs, snapshots), lib/volumes (persistent storage) and lib/network (the virtual network) are plain ES modules. See the repository and its docs. Licence: GPL-3.0-or-later.
